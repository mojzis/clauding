Practices on trial: in use in at least one repo, not in the [rules](../rules/v1.md) yet.
Adopt them by hand. A practice graduates by an ADR here and a new rules version;
until then it may change or be dropped.

| practice | lives in | on trial in | prevents |
|---|---|---|---|
| [session handoff](session-handoff.md) | `docs/next-session.md` + `docs/sessions/` | cecil, this repo | each session re-deriving where things stand |
| [ideas inbox](ideas-inbox.md) | `docs/ideas/` | cecil, this repo | ideas lost, or acted on mid-task |

To propose one: a page here with `status: trial`, the failure it prevents, where it's in use,
and open questions. Log what hurts while using it as [papercuts](../principles/papercuts.md).
